### Screen reader names on list pages (#2411)
- **Sort selects.** The per-page, sort and direction selects of the lists that have their own
  controls (users, activity, photos, posts, reviews, blogs, groups, categories and the admin
  lists) now have names: "Results per page", "Sort by", "Sort direction". The shared toolbar
  already had them.
- **Icon-only buttons.** The "..." menu on the event list, grid and calendar is "More actions".
  The eye buttons on the search page say which section they show or hide and whether it is
  open. The delete buttons on posts and threads, the edit link in the entity list, and the
  placeholder thumbnails of events, series, entities and users without a photo have names too.
- Nothing changes on screen.
